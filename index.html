Professional Website Overview

Website Purpose

The purpose of this website is to present my academic background, professional experience, and interdisciplinary skill set to potential employers.

With a foundation in advertising and communication, combined with training in information science and data analysis, this website serves as a professional portfolio that highlights my experience in internships, projects, and applied research. The site demonstrates how I translate data insights into strategic decisions and user-centered solutions.

By organizing my experiences in a clear and accessible format, the website allows employers to quickly understand my qualifications, technical interests, and long-term career goals in technology-driven roles.

Target Audience

The primary audience for this website is employers and recruiters seeking candidates for internships or entry-level professional roles at the intersection of technology, data, and communication.

This includes hiring managers and team leads in fields such as product management, data analysis, marketing technology, and information science.

  • Recruiters and hiring managers in technology-driven organizations
  • Teams seeking candidates with both analytical and communication skills
  • Employers evaluating portfolios for internships or entry-level roles
